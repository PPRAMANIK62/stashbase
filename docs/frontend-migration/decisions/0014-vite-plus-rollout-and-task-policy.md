# Roll out Vite+ as a focused and verifiable toolchain change

Vite+ migration precedes replacement scaffolding and proves the unchanged
Shipping application in a focused revertible change. Stable `pnpm` scripts
delegate to pinned Vite+ tasks, while Oxfmt receives a separate mechanical
baseline. Caching is limited to fully declared deterministic work and excluded
from native, E2E, visual, packaging, release, credentialed, and side-effecting
tasks. CI pins installation by commit and verifies a committed resolved-tool
inventory. After adoption Vite+ is the sole toolchain; rollback is reversion,
not permanent coexistence.
